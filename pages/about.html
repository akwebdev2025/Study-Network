OUR STORY

Useful technology.
Real student needs.

Study Network is a student-focused initiative with a simple aim: make everyday academic tasks easier through thoughtful, accessible digital tools.

Why Study Network?

Students regularly deal with small but time-consuming tasks — organising exam schedules, managing documents, calculating marks and finding useful study resources. Study Network brings these practical needs into one simple destination.

Our vision

We are starting with tools for Panjab University students and intend to grow thoughtfully, one useful feature at a time. The focus is not on having the most features; it is on making each feature genuinely helpful and easy to use.

What we believe

  • Student needs should guide what gets built.
  • Useful tools should be straightforward and accessible.
  • Clarity and reliability matter more than unnecessary complexity.
  • Feedback helps the platform improve.

Our tagline

Redefining Student Needs, Piece by Piece.
